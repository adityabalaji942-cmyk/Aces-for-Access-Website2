Who we are

Grounded in neuroscience, built on a court.

Prathiksai Amathi, Founder, age 15, Powell, Ohio · Founded 2026

Aces for Access was founded by Prathiksai Amathi, a varsity tennis player at Olentangy High School who spent his freshman year researching what chronic stress does to the teenage brain, work he's now extending at Ohio State University, with Department of Defense funding, into how sound affects PTSD treatment.

His research pointed to something concrete: adolescent stress causes measurable neurobiological damage, and physical activity like tennis directly lowers cortisol, protecting the developing brain against it.

"Put a racket in a kid's hand, teach them the game, and you give them more than a sport."

The catch was access. The kids who needed this most were the same kids who couldn't afford a single racket. So Prathik is building Aces for Access, a new 501(c)(3) nonprofit set to provide completely free tennis equipment, court access, and coaching to underserved youth across Columbus. We're just getting started, and our first clinics are still ahead of us.

What we do

Four commitments, every clinic.

Every session we run is built around the same four promises to the families who show up.

A

Offer evidence-based stress relief through physical activity via coaching.

B

Donate accessible equipment and court time, free of charge.

C

Employ dedicated, background-checked volunteer coaches.

D

Run personalized group clinics and one-on-one lessons.
