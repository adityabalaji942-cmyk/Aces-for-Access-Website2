Our donation

2,600 balls, 35 rackets, 150 kids.

We donated tennis equipment to Aces With Love Ohio to help support the kids they serve and keep more of them on the court.

We recently donated 2,600 tennis balls and 35 high-end rackets to Aces With Love Ohio. The equipment is meant for the kids they serve, about 150 of them, and for making tennis easier to get into.

Putting a price on it is a bit tricky. We estimate the donation at around $3,600 as it stands. If you bought everything new, the balls alone would come to roughly $5,000 and the rackets would add another $1,000.

The dollar amount matters less than what the equipment makes possible.

For a kid, that means more chances to practice, to get better, and to keep playing. That is what we care about, and it is why we wanted to get this gear to people who will use it.

Thank you to Aces With Love for the opportunity. Working with them has brought us much closer to our goal.

This is what Aces for Access is all about, and we are only getting started.
