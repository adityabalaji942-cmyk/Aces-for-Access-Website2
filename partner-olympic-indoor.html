A donation from Olympic Indoor.

Olympic Indoor made a donation to Aces for Access, and we are grateful for the support.

Olympic Indoor recently made a donation to Aces for Access, and we stopped in to say thank you in person.

Programs like ours depend on people who hear what we are trying to do and decide to help. Olympic Indoor did exactly that, and we appreciate it more than a quick thank-you can say.

Every donation, whatever the size, moves our first clinics a little closer.

Thank you to the Olympic Indoor team for believing in what we are building. We will keep you posted as things get going.
