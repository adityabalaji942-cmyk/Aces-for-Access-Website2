Partner story

Four boxes from Elysium Tennis.

Elysium Tennis donated 400 tennis balls to Aces for Access, and we could not be more thankful.

Sometimes a big boost shows up in a few cardboard boxes. Elysium Tennis in Columbus gave us 400 tennis balls, and they came in four boxes of Wilson US Open balls that we loaded into the car and brought home.

We stopped by the club to thank them in person. There were kids hitting on the courts right behind the pro shop while we were there, which is the kind of energy we want at our own clinics.

Every ball in those boxes is a little more time on the court for a kid who might not have had it.

The balls will support Buddy Up, which helps us make tennis more accessible and opens the door for more people to get involved in the sport. Having a steady supply also means more kids can hit at the same time, and fewer players standing around waiting for a turn.

Thank you to everyone at Elysium Tennis for backing us. We are grateful, and we plan to put every one of those balls to good use.
